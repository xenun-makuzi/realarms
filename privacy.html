Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how Liberty Arms handles personal information when you visit our website, contact our team, or interact with our services.

01. Information We Collect

Liberty Arms may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when using our website or communicating with our team.

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Information submitted through contact forms.
  • Account information when creating an account.
  • Information necessary to respond to customer enquiries.

02. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through our website may be used for legitimate business and customer service purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and customer requests.
  • Managing website accounts.
  • Providing requested services and support.
  • Maintaining website functionality.
  • Protecting our website against misuse and unauthorised access.

03. Information Sharing

Liberty Arms does not sell personal information as part of its stated website privacy practices.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate website features, process requests, maintain systems, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

04. Data Protection

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information submitted through our website against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

05. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, regulatory, and operational requirements.

06. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website or its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential website functionality and improve the browsing experience.

Browser settings may allow you to control or restrict certain cookies.

07. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have rights concerning the personal information we maintain about you.

These may include requesting access, correction, or deletion of eligible personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

08. Contact Us

For questions concerning this Privacy Policy or requests relating to personal information, please contact our team through the official Liberty Arms website.
